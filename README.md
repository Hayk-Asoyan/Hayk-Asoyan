## About

Frontend engineer with six years on product teams, mostly React, Next.js and TypeScript.

I work on the difficult parts of the front end: canvas rendering, contenteditable editors, real-time collaboration, and browser-heavy tooling. The kind of problem where the framework stops helping and you have to understand how the browser actually works. I build for performance and accessibility, and over the past year I have been shipping LLM features into production UI.

Currently Senior Frontend Engineer at **BeeWeb**.
